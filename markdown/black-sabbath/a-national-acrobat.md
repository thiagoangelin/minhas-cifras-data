This may not be the exact version, but is definitely a way 
 to play it. 
 
Tune all strings 2 frets down 
 
Used symbols : 
 
 v  - vibrato 
 h  - hammer next note 
 p  - pull off to next note 
 b  - bend to pitch next note 
 r  - release bend 
 \  - slide high to low/next note 
 /  - slide low to high/next note 
P.M.- palm muted
