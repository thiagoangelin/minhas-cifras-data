Bruce Dickinson - King In Crimson 
 
                                  "King in Crimson" 
                               (B. Dickinson, Roy Z.) 
 
              Guitar transcription by Aaron Shay (*) 
 
Ok...First of all,to play this song correctly you need 
at least one of two things : 
 
        a.) A guitar fit with bass strings and tuned 
            to drop A. 
 
        or 
 
        b.) A guitar in normal tuning with a Digitech 
            Whammy Pedal. (Or other unit that drops the 
            pitch one octave) 
 
        Since the chances are better (yet still slim) 
that some of you may have the second, I will tab it for 
normal guitar tuning. 
 
     In the darkness the... 
 
     In the courtyard... 
 
Repeat Riff B... 
 
Riff C...With Whammy Pedal 
                          No Pedal................... 
 
         Day light is    gone.... 
 
Repeat riff C... 
 
Repeat riff B... 
 
Riff D... Normal Octave (No Whammy Pedal) 
 
      As the veil reveals.... 
      As you scream into..... 
 
Chorus... With Whammy Pedal 
 
 Arise......Awake..The King in... 
 
Riff E...No Pedal 
 
Riff B (twice)... 
 
Riff D (twice)... 
 
Chorus (twice)... 
 
Riff E (twice)... 
 
This is all the riffs minus the solos. I might put the  
solos on a 
separate tab, but that is a lot of numbers.
