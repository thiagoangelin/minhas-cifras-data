Green Day - Letterbomb Broadway 
 
 
Tabbed by: Pie Borderie 
 
INFO - This version is the one from "American Idiot: The  
Original Broadway Cast Recording" since 
I couldn't find a tab of it anywhere else. The 997 power  
chord on the BOTTOM 3 strings can also 
be played as 442 on the 3rd 4th and 5th strings  
respectively. Sometimes this way is more convenient. 
 
ANECC TES - This is an interesting one from the album,  
most of the songs on here are either the same 
as the original (in terms of song key and notes played,  
not necessarily instrument wise.) or 
they, in great broadway fashion, move all the notes up one 
 or two frets. For example 
"Too Much Too Soon" starts 1 fret higher than the original 
 then moves up another 2 frets and 
ends on the original fretting. This one however as far as  
I can tell is quite different 
 
ENJOY!
