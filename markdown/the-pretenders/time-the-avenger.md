The Pretenders - Time The Avenger 
 
 
                                TIME THE AVENGER 
                     (From the 1983 Album LEARNING TO  
CRAWL) 
 
I have not tabbed much of the outro, as it is just  
variations on the D C chord change. 
lead guitar moves between sus2 and sus4 chord versions of  
each chord and occasionally 
a major 7th. Use of triplets and syncopation. I have  
included a mix of the parts that I enjoy.:) 
 
The chorus sounds a bit weird, due to the awkward changing 
 of time signatures. On most 
the guitar is obliterated by martin's crash cymbal. I got  
these few awkward bars partly 
watching Loose in LA and partly from the original. Its  
hard to get the feel for unless 
playing with other ppl, but its doable. :) 
 
Whilst watching the DVD for those bars I noticed that  
chrissie and adam play the main 
different ways of playing the same riff. The way I tabbed  
it is how adam plays the riff 
an alternative (chrissie's and bassist's way) I included  
in guitar 2 as a point of 
when the lead goes into thirds during verse 2. 
 
The main riff is fairly easy, as long as you take care  
thinking about up and down pick 
Both guitars use slight RH damping in places.

representation

moderation. Chrissie's part is 
crunched.
