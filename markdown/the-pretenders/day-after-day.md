Transcribed: (ugtc.* AT spamgourmet dot com) 
 
Great song, cleverly put together, use a nice rich  
overdrive with a humbucking treble pickup. 
 
Things to watch out for: 
 
The pseudo syncopation in the main riff (groups of 3 and  
2) JHS once attributed this common feature of 
 
early p's (the wait, tatooed love boys),  to Hynde's  
(presumably unintentional) disregard for normal 
 
timing. All notes are even length, but chords are accented 
 at the start of each group. 
 
Also let the notes ring, especially on the broken F#11  
chord at the end of the main riff and in the 
 
other broken chords. 
 
I have notated the various sections of the song as they  
appear chronologically but have not indicated 
 
the number of repeats. 
 
I've put in the bizzare outro lead, though im guessing  
performers wouldnt ever play it, if you want to 
 
turn it into an outro solo, use A and E chords underneath  
it. 
 
I've removed the notes durations, they clutter up the tab, 
 but i'll post a powertab version (free 
 
program) that has note durations. The notes in brackets  
are ties, dont play them! 
 
Played almost exclusively with downstrokes.
