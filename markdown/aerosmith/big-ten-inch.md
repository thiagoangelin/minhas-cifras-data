 pretty accurate 
but I'm still a bit cautious because it's the first time  
that I figured out 
a somewhat difficult solo. This is the solo on the album,  
by the way. I'm 
aware that their live versions(Unplugged,etc.)are a bit  
different in that 
they contain solos by Joe, Brad and Tom  
Ghimbel(saxophone)but this is the 
Toys in the Attic version and therefore, this is the only  
guitar solo. 
You'll have to work out the timing on your own because all 
 I did was put two 
spaces in between notes. Anyway, here it is:
