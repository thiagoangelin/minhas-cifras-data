Metallica - Kirk Hammett Guitar Lessons 
 
 
 /  Kirk Hammett Guitar Lessons  / 
/_______________________________/ 
 
I found these old blurry note sheets on the internet and  
rewrote them as tabs. 
The title of them was 'Kirk Hammett Guitar Lessons', and  
was supposed to be some 
rhythm figures that Kirk Hammett used for warm up before  
concerts. Well, the 
internet page was a Czech page, and the entire concept  
lacked a little of 
credibility, so I think I'd be a bit optimistic to believe 
 that these rhythm 
figures was either written or used by Kirk Hammett... But  
no offence, I used them 
for warm up, and they were great - just as great as any  
other thing you can play 
to heat up your fingers, but no there's really no  
musicality in them.
