There are two ways to tune the guitar to play this song.   
The way the band has 
it tuned (or at least James) on the S&M video is: Db,  
Ab, Db, Gb, Bb, eb 
(Dropped D down 1/2 step).  The song can also be played in 
 standard C# tuning. 
 
Music theory lesson for today:  C# and Db are the same  
note.
