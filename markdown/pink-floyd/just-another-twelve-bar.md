JUST ANOTHER TWELVE BAR 
Pink Floyd 
(Pink Floyd) 
 
This is a blues jam that the Floyd played live a few  
times, most notably on 
the "Smoking Blues" roio.  Like the title says, it's just  
a simple twelve bar. 
Fun to play, especially with 2 people: 1 rhythm, 1 solo.
