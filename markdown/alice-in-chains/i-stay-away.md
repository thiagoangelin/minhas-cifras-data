After finding the time and the capo I finally got to work  
on this 
song. It looks like there's 3 guitars in this song: 
 
Guitar 1 - a clean electric with a capo on the 5th fret 
Guitar 2 - a distorted electric with dropped-D tuning  
(DADGBE) 
Guitar 3 - just your average distorted guitar 
 
It's possible to play the solo with the dropped D (just  
raise fretnumbers 
on low E-string by 2) and the verse-chords with a  
finger-barre, but I can't 
advise that.. Here goes.. 
 
Notation 
 
XhY     - hammer on 
XpY     - pull off 
XbY     - bend at fret X to pitch of fret Y 
XrY     - release bent note at pitch of fret X to pitch of 
 fret Y 
X~~     - vibrato 
(X)     - ghost note 
X/Y X\Y - slides from X up/down to Y 
(...)   - hold previous note
