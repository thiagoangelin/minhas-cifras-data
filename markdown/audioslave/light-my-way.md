DADGBe 
 
Key: 
 
/= slide up

r= release 
o= open wah

Intro: 
Set your Digitech Whammy pedal up 2 octaves. 
Pick the G string behind the nut on your 
Fender Telecaster, and move the whammy pedal from 
toe down to toe up. Repeat two more times. 
 
Chorus: 
 
For the verse, turn your Dunlop Crybaby on, and open and close 
it to the beat of the song (ie, open on the quarter beats, 
closed on the &s).
