The Rolling Stones - Beast Of Burden Intro 
 
Date: 30 Nov. 04 
From: Grayson Carr 
 
I was looking around at tabs for this song and I found a  
much easier way to play it that 
actually sounds more like the song. Go ahead and play it  
while listening to the song and 
once you have the rhythm it sounds the exact same. I think 
 there are two guitars in the 
song but here's how to play it with one guitar. 
 
>------------------------------------------------------| 
>----5/7----7-7-7----5---------------------------------| 
>----6/7----7-7-7----6--9----------7-------------------| 
>----7/7----7-7-7----7--11----9h11---------------------| 
>------------------------------------------------------| 
>------------------------------------------------------| 
 
To make the first slide work, start your fingers in this  
position and slide the finger 
on the 5th fret to the 7th.
