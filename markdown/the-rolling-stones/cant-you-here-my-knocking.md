The Rolling Stones - Cant You Here My Knocking 
 
CAN'T YOU HEAR ME KNOKCING 
 
This song is divided in two parts, the first one is the  
rythm (by Keith) and the other is the solo (by Mick  
Taylor). 
The first part is in G tuning (DGDGBD) and the other is in 
 standard tuning. 
You should play the rythmic with a DoublePickup on a  
Telecaster (Custom) with the neck pickup. 
For the solo, it's a heavy LesPaul sound with sustain,  
played on the neck pickup too. 
For both sections, a valve amp is required... 
 
So here it is... 
 
b(1t): bend of one tone 
^v^v^v^v^v^v: vibrato 
s: slide 
po: pull off 
h: hammer on
