The White Stripes - Apple Blossom Live Solo 
 
Tuning: Standard (low to high: EADGBe) 
 
The rest of the song is the same as CD version but electric 
and with a different solo.  
This is from "Under Black Pool Lights" DVD 
 
Jack uses a whammy pedal to gain his unique sound for  
this solo, but if you don't have one, the best way  
to achieve this sound is to use the bridge pickup  
with tone knob turned all the way to zero. 
 
(After second verse) 
 
End 
 
Key 
~ - Vibrato 
h - hammer on
