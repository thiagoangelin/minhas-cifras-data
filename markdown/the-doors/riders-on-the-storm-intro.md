The Doors - Riders On The Storm Intro 
 
Standard Tuning 
 
This is an intro I like to play when there is no organ.   
If you have a delay with a 
hold function, you can lay down the bass line and play  
this over it and it sounds 
awesome.  The ascending e minor scale can be a bit tricky  
to get down but its awesome 
when you finally get it.  Also, if you want to jam around  
in the beginning, use the e 
minor, harmonic minor, and pentatonic minor scale.  This  
is my first tab that I've 
posted on here, so feel free to send comments and  
corrections.  Have fun!
