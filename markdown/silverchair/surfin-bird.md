Surfin' Bird is a cover of the Trashmen and the Cramps,  
recorded in less than two 
hours in Sydney, New South Wales, Australia during late  
January 1996. It appears on 
MOM, a compilation which is a benefit for the Surfrider  
Foundation. Very basic song 
with three chords. I think the bass follows the guitar  
except in the second riff.
