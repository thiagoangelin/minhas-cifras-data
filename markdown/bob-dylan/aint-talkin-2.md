Great alternate version: different key, different lyrics. 
The song was reworked and finished for "Modern Times". 
The "Modern Times" recording is in Abm and the second  
chord 
in the verses (here D7) is indeed a minor chord. The major 
 chord 
in this early version sounds strange and beautiful. Enjoy!
