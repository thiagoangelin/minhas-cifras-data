These are the chords played on the studio version from  
“Freewheelin’”. 
The key is E, but here I suggest using a Drop-D tuning  
with a capo 2nd fret. 
Drop D is easy to do: Just tune down the 6th string (low  
E) down two steps 
to D and leave the other strings as they are. 
 
What makes Dylan's usage of Drop D tuning interesting is  
the continuous drone 
in the verses. I made a quick demo recording, so you can

the slashes - instead
