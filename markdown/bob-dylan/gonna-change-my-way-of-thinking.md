Bob Dylan - Gonna Change My Way Of Thinking 
 
 
Written by: Bob Dylan 
From: “Slow Train Coming” (1979) 
 
Tabbed by: maguri 
Tuning: Standard 
 
The song follows a blues pattern.  As far as I can tell,  
the guitars do not 
play the 3rd, so strictly speaking the chords are A5, D5  
and E5 (see tab). 
 
I put the basic chords with the lyrics for novice players. 
 These chords, 
however, aren’t really played, the accompaniment is mostly 
 riff (see tab). 
Only here and there, there's an organ playing the actual  
chords and from 
the organ one can tell that all the chords are actually  
major chords and 
can be played as A7, D7 and E7.  Playing the riff and  
singing at the same 
time is more of an intermediate job.
