Radiohead - Last Flowers 
 
 
Tabbed by:      b34r5 
 
Tuning:         Standard 
 
The song is also known as 'Last flowers till the  
hospital', 'Cogs', 
and 'Glass flowers' (According to greenplastic). It was  
written in the days of OK 
Computer but played acoustic and live by Thom at a trade  
justice demo 
earlier this year (which me and a few friends went to..  
but missed the 
performance >:'( 
 
It is rumoured to appear on the fourthcoming album. 
 
The lyrics are open to interpretation and my version is  
most definately wrong, but 
Thom will probably change them for the album version if  
there is one. The chords are 
almost there, though they are picked in the song (almost  
ala 'no suprises') so it 
sounds different. If you do pick, you'll have to play the  
E#m in an Am shape higher 
up the fretboard (see E#m2). You'll then have to slide it  
up to play the next Gm chord. 
 
Timing is very very tricky, hence some of the words are  
spaced out. Also, 
there are some more minor chord alterations I left out  
because the ammount of 
chords was just becoming silly. Also my formatting is all  
wrong, as it doesnt 
really reflect the structure of the song, but it does make 
 fitting the chords 
in easier. 
 
Any comments, corrections, questions or alterations and  
please drop me a mail 
or correct on here.
