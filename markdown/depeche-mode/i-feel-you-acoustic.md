Depeche Mode - I Feel You Acoustic 
 
Once i spent some time jaming on my acoustic and found a  
good way to play this song. 
Mode songs make good acoustic versions - Personal Jesus is 
 one of my favourites! 
 
The song is in Am 
 
Main riff: 
 
This Riff Is very simple. Just play the A5 on the 6th  
string and add the C on the 8th 
with your little finger. You can also play the full chord  
but it doesn't sound better. 
 
Chorus: 
 
This part may be the most difficult. If you're good at  
chords, you'll pick it quickly. 
trickiest part is the bridge from C5 to main riff. Notice  
the slides.
