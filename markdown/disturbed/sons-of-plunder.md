Legend - I suggest looking over it, there are some  
descriptions, as well as special cases. 
 
 /  - Slide Right (If only 1 number just slide from  
anywhere towards it left to right) 
 \  - Slide left (If only 1 number just slide from  
anywhere towards it right to left) 
 ^  - Bend (5^7= bend the 5 fret until it sounds like 7,  
or 5^ = quick bend) 
(~) - Vibrato (5(~) = Wiggle the string shown) 
(5) - Natural Harmonic (Just put your finger lightly over  
the actually fret) 
<5> - Artificial Harmonic (You pick the string and  
let your thumb touch as well while 
holding the fret shown) 
 x  - Mute that string 
 .  - Palm Mute (Mute the note shown with picking hand  
palm, the '.' is under the note) 
 h  - Hammer On (ie..3h5 = pic the 3rd fret and then  
hammer your finger into the 5th without picking) 
 p  - Pull Off (opposite of hammer on, 5p3 = pic the 5th  
while holding the 3rd as well, 
then pull your finger of the 5th) 
=#= - Ghost note (A note that you don't NEED to play or is 
 played quietly) 
 
W - whole 
H - half 
Q - quarter 
E - 8th 
S - 16th 
T - 32nd 
X - 64th 
. - note dotted 
|-n-|  - n-tuplets 
L - Tied Note
