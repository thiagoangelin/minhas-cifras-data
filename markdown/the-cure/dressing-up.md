I love this song, any time I hear it, I think of my friend 
 Annita for some 
reason (I have only known her a couple months).  Anyway, I 
 am transcribing 
the -Paris- version of the song.  It really does not make  
that much of a 
difference since I have heard the original and it sounds  
exactly like 
the -Paris- version.  I broke the song up into two riffs  
like these:

   Bm             C       D              E5  Em

   C                 Bm          C                 Bm
