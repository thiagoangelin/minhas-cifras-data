Kiss - Love Gun 
 
Tuned down 1/2-step 
 
Comments: This isn't quite tuned to Eb, it's a smidgen  
lower. The easy way 
to get past that is to tune to the album. Not much happens 
 on this track. The 
riff stays pretty much the same throughout. I'm not sure  
whether Rhy. Fig 2b, 
which is definitely there during the intro, is also played 
 during the chorus 
or if both guitars play the same thing there. When the  
harmony guitars turn 
up towards the end I'm not 100% but I think that the  
second harmony (starting 
on the 19th fret) is doubled an octave down (which would  
then be the same as 
the first harmony).
