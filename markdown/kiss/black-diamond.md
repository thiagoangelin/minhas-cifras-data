Black Diamond is one of the best known KISS songs of all  
time. It has been used for years as 
the ending of almost every KISS show. This version  
features the decending pitch at the end of 
the song. Ace bases his leads on the A minor blues scale.  
He uses some cool string skipping and 
pull-offs and hammer-ons to improvise on the basic blues  
positions. This creates some great 
lead work and easy to remember lines.
