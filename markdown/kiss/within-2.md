Drop-D tuning down a whole-step 
 
Comments: A monster of a tuning this. I recommend a  
set-neck guitar or a 
through-body. String it with at least 11s otherwise the  
low E-string (now 
C-string) will go out of tune in a second. Rhy. Figs 1a  
and 1b seem to be 
backwards as are Riffs 2, 3a and 3b. They have been  
notated here as they 
sound.

/    = slide up 
\    = slide down 
b    = bend (whole step) 
b^   = bend (1/2 step) 
b^^  = bend (1 1/2 steps) 
pb   = pre-bend 
r    = release-bend 
t    = tap with righthand finger 
h    = hammer-on 
p    = pull-off 
~    = Vibrato 
*    = Natural Harmonic 
#(#) = Trill 
**   = Artificial Harmonic 
x    = Dead notes (no pitch) 
P.M. = Palm mute (- -> underneath indicates which  
notes) 
(\)  = Dive w\bar 
(/)  = Release w\bar 
Tp   = Tap w\plectrum
