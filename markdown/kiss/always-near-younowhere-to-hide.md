Kiss - Always Near Younowhere To Hide 
 
 
Transcribed by Emil Ortenmark, * 
 
Standard tuning 
 
Comments: I like these chords, specially when the  
"harmony" part comes in 
by the second verse. There is also some very good solo  
guitar work here 
(although the last G natural against the E major chord in  
Riff 3 is a little 
annoying even with the gradual bend to the A). The high  
A's that fade in 
(both bent and straight) give a slightly eerie feel to the 
 track. And you 
got to love Gene's castrato vocals during the jamming  
outro.

h    = hammer-on 
p    = pull-off 
~    = Vibrato 
*    = Natural Harmonic 
#(#) = Trill 
**   = Artificial Harmonic 
x    = Dead notes (no pitch)

notes)
