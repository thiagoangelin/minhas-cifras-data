Kiss - 100000 Years 
 
Eb Tuning 
 
Key: 
/    = slide up 
\    = slide down 
b    = bend (whole step) 
b^   = bend (1/2 step) 
pb   = pre-bend 
r    = release-bend 
h^   = hammer-on 
p^   = pull-off 
~    = Vibrato 
*    = Natural Harmonic 
#(#) = Trill 
**   = Artificial Harmonic 
x    = Muted String (Palm Muting) 
X    = Muted String (Fretboard Muting) 
 
100,000 years one of the finest tracks ever recorded by  
KISS in my opinion. 
It was also a REAL bitch to transcribe since some of the  
rhythm parts are 
buried a bit in the mix. The Rhythm parts here should do  
the job to play 
this song fairly accurately. Even listening to Double  
Platinum and ALIVE! 
didn't really help me with this tune. Ace plays it totally 
 different live, 
he skips a few passages in the solo and outro that I have  
transcribed here 
pretty close. Ace's solo and outros are basically the  
same, wild repeated 
unison bends in three different timings. Pay attention to  
those unison 
bends at the beginning, I have given approximate number of 
 times to bend, 
but LISTEN and play with feel. Try not to count it, it  
will help the feel of 
the solo immensely. Paul plays sparse throughout and Ace  
fills in during 
some verses with doublestops to move the song along. This  
is a song with 
great playing, excellent vocals and a great arrangement..  
A KISS classic!
