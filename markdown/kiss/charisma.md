/    = slide up 
\    = slide down 
b    = bend (whole step) 
b^   = bend (1/2 step) 
b^^  = bend (1 1/2 steps) 
pb   = pre-bend 
r    = release-bend 
r^   = release-bend half-step 
h^   = hammer-on 
p^   = pull-off 
~    = Vibrato 
*    = Natural Harmonic 
#(#) = Trill 
**   = Artificial Harmonic 
x    = Muted String (Palm Muting) 
X    = Muted String (Fretboard Muting) 
(#)  = Tap w/ Right Hand 
 
This song is kind of a forgotten track on a pretty good  
album. Yes, it does 
have a disco feel but the songs are pretty good. This solo 
 might be Gene or 
Ace, but some of this work leads me to believe it's at  
least in part Ace. 
One cool aspect of this solo is the right hand tapping  
(ala Eddie Van Halen). 
Obviously KISS used this influence after hearing (and  
knowing) Eddie and 
the rest of Van Halen from their 1978 release and before  
their signing.
