Kiss - Is That You 
 
 
Standard tuning 
 
Comments: This track, although not penned by a  
KISS-member, pretty much sets 
the tone of this album. It is pop-city here but with some  
nasty-sounding 
guitars. If I had to venture a guess, I would say that the 
 solo is Paul. 
Frankly, I don't even see Ace anywhere near this track.  
There is something 
in the background during the second verse. I think it's an 
 electric piano 
playing staccato eights. The most interesting thing abouot 
 this track is the 
very weird little fill that appears in the first chorus  
(Riff 1). It is 
totally out of left field (which makes it appropriate that 
 it appears in 
the left channel).
