In the bridge when the lyrics stop the song has a very  
rhythmical guitar part before the solo. 
This is the bit in the tab which begins on the first  
"....tonight" and is made up firstly 
of barred E's & D's and then moves down through D's  
& C's to Bm and A. It's starting to 
sound complicated but trust me, it ain't that hard if you  
play along with the CD for the 
rhythm.
