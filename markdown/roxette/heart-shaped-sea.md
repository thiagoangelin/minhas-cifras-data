Dx should be played as follows: 
 
Dx: 
 
 0-IIIIII   IIIIII   IIIIII   IIIIII 
   III*I*   III*I*   III*II   III*I* 
   IIII*I   IIII**   IIII*I   IIII*I 
      2x       2x      2x        1x 
 
So you strum the normal D chord twice, then add the g-note and 
strum twice, then release the e-string altogether and finally 
you return to the normal D chord.
