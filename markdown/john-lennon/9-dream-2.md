John Lennon - 9 Dream 2 
 
 
Written by: John Lennon 
From: "Walls and Bridges" (1974), now on many Greatest  
Hits collections 
 
Tabbed by: maguri 
Tuning: Standard 
 
One of Lennon's sweetest tunes. Large parts of the  
composition and the 
production sound very much like Harrison - from the slide  
guitar intro 
to the chord pattern in the chorus and outro recalling "My 
 Sweet Lord".
