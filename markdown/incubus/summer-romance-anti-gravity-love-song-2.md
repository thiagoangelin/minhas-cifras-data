Guitar Setup: 
Use the neck pickups to get a warm a sound as you can.  
Then put it 
through some type of tube overdrive. On a Line6 "Pod" - I  
used "Tube 
Preamp" - then set "Drive" to about 70% and added the  
compressor. 
 
Playing: 
The little riff at the end of Phrase I uses a traditional  
jazz guitar 
playing technique. To play this phrase, use your index and 
 pinky 
(or index and ring) fingers to play the octave notes. Use  
your index 
finger to muffle the string in between. For example: to  
play an octave 
of "D" (10th fret - high E and 7th fret G string). Put  
your index on 
the 7th fret (G string) let your index finger lay on top  
of the B string 
and put your ring or pinky on the 10th fret and STRUM the  
G, B and E strings. 
You should only hear the 2 fretted notes - and the B  
string should be 
muffled out. This is a good technique to learn in general  
- it's used a lot 
in modern jazz. The rest of it should be self-explanatory.
