Incubus - Under My Umbrella 
 
Under My Umbrella was surprisingly a difficult song to  
tab, because of all the slight changes in chords. 
The little beeping sound after the intro and all the way  
through the verse is actually DJ Kilmore, so don't 
worry, that isn't on the guitar. At first I thought that  
was the guitar, but then I realized that Mike Einziger 
actually plays the melody and plays while Brandon is  
singing, but at no other time during the verse. The first 
part of the chorus is the same as the intro, but don't be  
fooled: the second part is slightly different!
