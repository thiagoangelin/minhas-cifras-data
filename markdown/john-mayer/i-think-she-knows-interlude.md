John Mayer - I Think She Knows Interlude 
 
 
Tabbed by: Thomas Favale 
 
This is a TAB to the famous cover of I Think 
She Knows that John Mayer uploaded to his 
personal YouTube channel a while back. Feel 
free to email me with any questions. Enjoy! 
 
Tuning: Standard 
 
Closing Lick: 
This is probably the most intricate part of the cover, 
but it really brings it to the next level. I'll try to 
describe what John's doing to the best of my ability. 
He starts with his ring finger on the 14th fret of the 
G string, but is also holding down the 12th fret on the 
same string with his index finger (he does this because 
later in the lick he uses his ring finger to pull-off). 
Next, he slides from the 14th fret of the G string to 
the 16th fret of the G string (his index finger is now 
on the 14th fret). Then, he barres the 15th fret on the 
B and high E strings with his middle finger and uses his 
index finger (picking hand) to sweep downward across the 
high E, B, and G strings. If it's difficult to get the 
15th fret on the B string, you can just place your middle 
finger on the 15th fret of the high E string, use your 
index finger that's holding down the 14th fret of the G 
string to mute the B string, and then perform the sweep. 
This is what I find the easiest to do, but it still sounds 
fantastic. Immediately upon hitting the G string, he pulls 
-off his ring finger (16p14), then slides his index finger 
(remember, we kept the index finger here for the whole  
first 
half of the lick) down to the 12th fret of the G string  
(14\12). 
He uses his ring finger to hit the 14th fret of the G  
string 
again, adds some vibrato, and then pulls off to the 12th  
fret 
of the G string (12-14~p12). He adds a little variation  
the 
first time he does this (see the second part of  
Transitional 
Riff #2 above), but then repeats the same lick 12 times  
afterward. 
The 13th time, he plays the lick and then quickly runs  
down and 
then back up the E minor pentatonic scale, but something  
more 
simple would certainly suffice. 
 
E Minor Pentatonic Lick: 
This is how I end the song. Remember, 
it's used IMMEDIATELY after the previous 
lick, and it's DA LLY fast. Consult the 
video on YouTube for more clarity. I've 
included the 13th repeat of the previous 
lick below so the transition is more apparent.
