In the song, Jimmy Page has recorded the song with Guitar  
1 (Clean) to begin with 
then dubbed in Guitar 2 (Mild distortion) to emphasize  
various parts and then gone 
over it all again with Guitar 3 (Heavy fuzz) to stress  
chords in the Chorus and give 
the solo an edge. 
If you don’t have the ability of having six hands, then  
it’s best to put the guitar 
parts together and come up with your own variation. Enjoy!
