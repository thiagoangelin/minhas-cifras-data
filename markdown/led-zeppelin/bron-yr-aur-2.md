HINTS 
h = hammer-on 
    (i.e. 0h3 = play open string then hammer-on to 3rd  
fret) 
p = pull-off 
    (i.e. 3p0 =  play at 3rd fret then pulloff to open  
string) 
    (4h5p4 = play at 4th fret, hammer-on to 5th fret,  
pulloff to 4th fret. 
     Also called a trill) 
s = slide 
    (i.e. 5s7 =  play at 5th fret then slide to 7th fret) 
x = rest beat 
    (Be aware of the rest (x) at the end of many segments  
which allows 
     for cleaner chord and position changes.) 
* = placed over a beat that has a something explained to  
the right

 (i=index, m=middle, r=ring, p=pinky, 0=open)
