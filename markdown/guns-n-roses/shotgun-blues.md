Performance Notes:- This is a great song with Slash doing  
both rhythm and lead guitars with  Izzy only doing  
background vocals. Axl also played rhythm guitar on this  
song.  The intro features some microphonic feedback and a  
pick slide. The chords to this song might look rather  
daunting, but, if you practice hard enough, you will get  
the hang of it. The trick between the chord changes in the

 chorus is to play a power chord and then to just keep  
your first finger on the 6th string and just lay your  
third finger on the 2nd, 3rd and 4th strings. At first,  
this will be quite difficult but keep practising and it  
will become easier.  The pre-chorus features the use of  
sliding power chords which lend a slight punk rock feel to 
 this part of song.  The bridge uses strummed chords that  
lead up to the guitar solo. Strum these chords in a  
down/up motion. The guitar solo features high bends and  
some fast hammer-ons and pull-offs. Practice this (it will 
 pay off in the long run). Listen to the record to  
determine how Slash is feeling the phrase against the  
beat. There is some tough phrasing in the guitar solo so  
analyse the lines carefully and listen to the record.
