This song is known as either Crazy Fool (Pure Anus  
bootleg) or Foolish Fool 
(Everything Under The Sun: Sublime 
Rarities Boxset and Acoustic: Bradley Nowell & Friends 
 ), multiple versions exist. 
The differences are minor and you will be able to figure  
them out with this tab and the optional 
chords; which would be used as one stroke passing chords). 
 Play around with up and down strokes. 
BTW, the music would later be used for the song 
'Get Ready' from the band's 1996 eponymous album.
