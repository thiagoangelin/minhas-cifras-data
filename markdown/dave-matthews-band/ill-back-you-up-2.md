The Legend: 
() soft notes 
^  bend 
~  vibrato 
h  hammer on 
p  pull off 
!  optional note (played so fast it could be left out) 
_  tie 
 
The carets and periods along the bottom are timing  
markers. 
 
This should all be correct (if it isn't, then it sounds  
pretty close!)
